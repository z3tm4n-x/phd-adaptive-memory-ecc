## Executor and unchanged controller

One word slot 200 ns; P=0.1048576 s. Parallel 48-bit interface. First 100 ns belongs to backlogged best-effort application reads; last 100 ns reserved for service. Read occupies first 20 ns of reservation; latch is 80 ns before commit; decode/control takes 40 ns; conditional full-word write occupies [commit−40 ns,commit−20 ns]; acknowledgement at commit. Chosen timings are a calculational interface contract with margin to the part's 10 ns grade, not measured FPGA timing or board WCET. External arbitration is deterministic, no unbounded contention. Dirty latch produces a corrected snapshot write and one count at acknowledged completion, including parity; clean latch does not write. A later inversion can be overwritten by the snapshot. During the gap E_cap is still detected. Simultaneous priority: commit, atomic group, latch. Later toggle/reset cannot undo E_cap. Groups are not serialized into fictitious intermediate bit errors.

A period a consists of idle a−P then sequential pass. Last chosen full end can exceed H: execute only events/operations through H, no count observation for incomplete pass. No hourly reset. Service stop-cost ends exactly at first E_cap; separately report own survival and joint-survival resource. Hidden E_cap only terminates accounting, is not an input to controller choice. Application data writes are outside this declared workload.

Controller retains accepted core.py and simulate.py decision/observation/analogue functions, with new W=524288, n=39 bookkeeping, P, H, rates, D=60, backup=.5 s; U and caps K12/C32 unchanged. Completed count saturates at category32 as in accepted kernel (not dropping mass). No compound-count kernel or new filter. Reference potential .06114977424118347, delta .00033146425961188614, initial slack .03851876149920464. Numerical allowance .0003 covers independently re-evaluated SR §5 affine-guard ledger; all remainders are recalculated. These are conditional singleton/instantaneous-model bounds, NOT grouped/delayed physical bounds. Delay/model mismatch is directly simulated. Zero likelihood, lost backup or nonfinite update aborts with seed/case/trial/policy; no hidden fallback or discarded trial. Original paid 1e-13 action tolerance/max(0,slack−D) remains.
