# Model scope and engineering resource contract

The full parameter table and source/assumption distinctions are in PREREGISTRATION.md and config.json. These are fixed model conditions, not a flight subsystem specification. Source provenance is inputs/manifest.json; all 60 supplied classified group lists match independent connected-component extraction exactly.

## What the neutron reconstruction preserves

For each static test: exact addresses and XOR mismatch masks; bit count; word count; native double-word occurrences. The main model also preserves the archive's per-test 35-offset XOR connected components and their distribution when selecting unshifted templates. Spatial translation deliberately changes absolute hotspots, while preserving all intratemplate XOR relationships. Neither model claims to reproduce the law of the original final registration map after repeated exposure: toggles, missed events, grouping accidents and true parent times remain unidentified. Firmware MIEDO-SRAM-2/main.cpp, `read_full_memory` (lines1071–1109 in supplied source), reads each word, prints differing read/expected values, then writes its expected pattern back. It supplies no timestamps per incident particle and is not our ECC-count channel.

The alternative joins the nine double-bit native words. There are exactly nine resulting templates each containing exactly one native double-word; no double-word templates overlap one another. Thus direct-hit counts are not accidentally counted twice. No physical equivalence of alternative parent processes is asserted.

SOURCE facility normalization is adopted as reported. Whole-test resampling treats the 60 records as exchangeable and does not include calibration error, device-to-device variation, or unobserved high multiplicities. Registered endpoints are reconstructed as toggles, not measured event cross sections. Flux=fluence/time and reconstructed response=groups/fluence cancel to groups/time for the chosen high exposure. All-chip protected bit rate is derived independently from total differing bits, never from parent rate alone. Applying 39/64 to the bit rate is not a fit.

## Why the merged-model diagnostic is conservative

Consider only the nine templates with two distinct positions at a native word on chip0 or chip1, ignoring the parity chip and all other words. Common uniform address XOR puts the selected word in the active quarter with probability 1/4. Under survival, it contains at most one erroneous protected bit. For a fixed existing erroneous data bit, at most 2 of the 16 independent common lane XORs include that bit in the incoming pair. At least 7/8 therefore cause E_cap, regardless of policy/history; clean words and an existing bit on another chip only increase this probability. Atomic application is essential to this statement.

Consequently a conservative conditional killing intensity is r_H=(9/3119.5)*2*(1/4)*(7/8), r_L=.1*r_H. The probability lower bound in this **declared reconstruction** is 1−(1/2,1/2) exp((Q−diag(r_L,r_H))*H) 1. This does not infer a physical parent rate from static counts. The bootstrap and optional Poisson/exposure interval on nine observed templates are explicit input sensitivities, not new physical confidence bounds. More frequent scans cannot remove this declared direct mechanism; they may still change accumulated-error risk.

## Full-word executor and observation

The previous package's decoder/counter regressions apply after the stated systematic bit permutation: one error anywhere among 39 is corrected/counts, two distinct positions exceed guaranteed SEC capability. Count is one per acknowledged corrected word, not number of raw impacts or toggles. No ECC functionality is assumed inside ISSI BLL. No information bits/check bits distinction in sensitivity is measured; equal response is an architecture/model assumption of identical storage cells/chips.

At commit the corrected latched snapshot overwrites all 39 protected positions. This differs from an instantaneous reset at latch and can overwrite an inversion arriving during the 80 ns gap. E_cap remains absorbing even if the later write would clear it. A clean read produces no corrective write. Byte/word accesses of a different historical executor are not inherited: parallel chips give one 48-bit interface read and at most one conditional 48-bit write per protected word. Padding is physically transferred but not in E_cap. Events on inactive/padding cells stay outside the modeled buffer.

Complete pass: W reads; writes depend on own dirty latches. Partial interval: exact completed reads, acknowledged writes, read/write bus duration and fractional service reservation up to stop. A write in progress at E_cap contributes busy time even if not yet acknowledged. Guard/decoding/ack slots are reserved service time, not SRAM signal-active time. Peak: service reserves 50% of a continuously scanning slot sequence and can own 100% instantaneously during its transactions. Application obtains all unreserved slots; no claim about latency for a different application-write workload. During each pass, backlogged demand loses half the slot capacity; outside passes it can use all capacity. A blocking best-effort arrival just after a service window starts waits at most 100 ns, apart from its own queued application backlog.

## Controller resource vector and break-even condition

Tables reside in separate protected control memory/cache; q (26 doubles), slack, counters and timing state reside in controller working storage. Correctness/availability of these components is assumed and NOT covered by E_cap of the data buffer. If placed on the same contested SRAM bus, their traffic must be charged and the declared executor would need re-evaluation.

Count-enabled observation reads at most 26*26=676 binary64 coefficients and performs 676 products/additions plus normalization per completed pass. Count-disabled uses the marginal transition with the same dense dimensions, but its deterministic belief evolution can be precomputed; the current measurement does not assume that optimization. Each chosen action is found by at most 12 affine evaluations using moment summaries and potential coefficients; actual number of candidates varies. PA-DOM needs scalar history/arithmetic, no belief/kernel tables. Fixed needs one period; Precomputed two periods and a time comparison. Host timings are descriptive, NOT a claimed board WCET.

Full reference table footprint and packed exploitation footprint are measured from nbytes, separate from compressed archive size. Offline preparation is not charged per mission. Mean saved service reservation on joint survivors is an upper allowance for additional controller occupation of the *same resource*. A positive lower confidence bound divided by the Proposed update count supplies a conservative allowable extra seconds/update at the observed mean update count (the denominator is descriptive, not its own simultaneous bound). No finite worst-case latency, net energy saving, or total hardware cost is inferred. A deployment with controller traffic/computation exceeding this allowance has not demonstrated net interface benefit. Its own working/table memory is a separate price even if timing fits.

Decision computation can occupy the beginning of the already scheduled idle interval, without shifting any check, provided its latency is at most a−P. The uniform minimum deadline is 0.2−0.1048576=0.0951424 s, including initial choice. This is an explicit executor/controller placement condition, not a measured deadline proof. A deadline miss invalidates the declared schedule and must not be hidden by shifting epochs. The interface break-even allowance and this causal deadline are different constraints. The scalar controller is assumed to meet the deadline in this calculational execution; host microbenchmark provides only plausibility, not certification of a specific processor.
