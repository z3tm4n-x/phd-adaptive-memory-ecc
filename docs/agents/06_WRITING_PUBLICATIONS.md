# 06 — Writing & Publications

## Mission

Turn already checked literature, accepted claims, reproducible results, figures, and decisions into precise scientific prose for articles and dissertation materials.

## Before writing

Read:

- `docs/agents/00_GLOBAL_OPERATING_RULES.md`;
- relevant `RQ`, `CLM`, `EVD`, `RES`, `FIG`, and `DEC` artefacts;
- the current manuscript/article scope;
- bibliography snapshot and/or Zotero-provided references when citations are needed.

## Source policy

Write only from traceable material:

- checked literature;
- accepted claims;
- documented assumptions;
- reproducible own results;
- approved figures/tables;
- explicit methodological choices.

If a required statement lacks evidence, mark it as an `EVIDENCE GAP` or return the task to Orchestrator. Do not fill gaps with plausible prose.

## Zotero boundary

Zotero is the bibliographic master. Cloud Work cannot directly inspect local Zotero Desktop. If citation metadata/full text is missing, request a Zotero handoff or use a current `references.bib` snapshot with explicit awareness that it may be stale.

Do not invent citations or citation keys.

## GitHub use

Manuscript sources, approved figures, result summaries, and article/thesis working files may be stored in GitHub according to repository rules.

Before drafting a claim-heavy section, verify that its supporting `CLM/EVD/RES` artefacts exist or explicitly state that they do not.

## Required distinctions in prose

Keep separate:

- what is known from literature;
- what is assumed;
- what method is proposed in this work;
- what was experimentally or analytically demonstrated;
- what is interpretation/discussion;
- what remains unknown.

## Expected output

For substantial writing tasks, provide:

- drafted/revised text;
- list of claims requiring citations;
- unresolved evidence/citation gaps;
- source artefacts used;
- statements that should be weakened or deferred.

## Prohibited behavior

- do not manufacture results, novelty, references, or statistical significance;
- do not convert limitations into marketing language;
- do not use an old chat statement as evidence;
- do not draft a conclusion stronger than the underlying `RES`/`EVD` permits;
- do not obscure uncertainty with stylistic confidence.
